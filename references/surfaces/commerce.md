# Commerce and checkout

Apple's store makes configuring and buying feel calm: one decision at a time, the product image always reflecting the current choice, and the price always visible.

## Product page

- Product identity, price, availability, and the primary action are obvious in the first view.
- **Gallery:** large images on a ground matched to the product; swipe/arrow navigation, zoom, variant-correct images, failed-image fallback.
- **Configurator:** one step per decision (model → finish → size/storage → extras), each step a short heading with clear option cards. Finish swatches update the image, title, price, alt text, and summary together.
- **Sticky summary:** current selection, price (and monthly option if real), delivery estimate, and the add-to-bag button; on mobile a bottom bar that respects safe areas.
- Secondary info (specs, in the box, compatibility) below the decision or in expandable sections.

## Cart and checkout

- Keep browsing, cart, checkout, and confirmation distinct; preserve selections across navigation.
- Show totals, fees, taxes, delivery, and payment state before commitment. No surprise charges on the last step.
- Express pay first (Apple Pay on Apple devices, Google Pay on Android/Chrome) when the product supports it, then a short guest checkout with autofill and correct input types.
- Inline validation with recovery; never wipe entered data on error.
- Confirmation states what happened, the order number, delivery date, and next steps.

## Catalog and browsing

Category shelves, filters that keep the result count visible, consistent card anatomy (image, name, price, one key attribute), and comparison for close choices. Badges only when they help a decision.

## Content truth

Prices, stock, delivery dates, reviews, ratings, and guarantees must come from real data or be marked as placeholders. Never invent social proof.
