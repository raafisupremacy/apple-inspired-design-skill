# Copy and voice

Apple-inspired copy is short, confident, human, and specific. It sells one idea at a time and lets the visuals do the rest. Write in the product's own language and voice; these patterns work in English and Indonesian alike.

## Marketing patterns

- **Headline:** 2–6 words, one claim, and **specific to this product** — name the concrete thing it does or the concrete result. Wordplay only when it is about the product itself. Generic parallel or rhyming slogans ("Move more. Stress less.", "Work smarter, not harder") are AI-slop tells; so are hype and exclamation marks. Test: could a competitor use this line unchanged? Then rewrite it.
- **Subhead:** one sentence that explains the benefit in plain words, often with one concrete number.
- **Eyebrow:** the product or feature name, or "New".
- **CTA:** a clear verb: "Buy", "Try it free", "Learn more", "Download". One primary and at most one secondary.
- **Proof:** specific numbers with footnote markers and sources. Never invent ratings, reviews, certifications, or performance figures; mark illustrative copy as illustrative.
- **Section rhythm:** each section answers one question — what is it, why it matters, how it works, what it costs, why trust it.

Examples (pattern, not wording to copy):

```text
Generic   Headline: Focus better. Achieve more.            (fits any productivity app)
Specific  Headline: Only your people get through.        Subhead: Focus Mode silences every notification except the contacts you pick.
ID        Headline: Hanya orang pilihanmu yang bisa masuk. Subhead: Mode Fokus membungkam semua notifikasi, kecuali dari kontak yang kamu pilih.
```

## Interface copy

- Labels are concrete nouns or verbs: "Save", "Add Card", "Delete Photo" — not "Submit", "OK", "Process".
- Follow the platform's and product's capitalization convention and apply it consistently.
- **Errors:** what happened + what to do next, without blame. "Couldn't connect. Check your connection and try again."
- **Empty states:** say what will appear here and offer the first action. A friendly illustration is optional; the action is not.
- **Permissions:** explain the benefit before the system prompt; ask at the moment of need.
- **Success:** state what changed ("Payment sent to Rina"), not just "Success!".
- **Destructive actions:** name the object and the consequence; offer undo where possible.

## Content truth

Separate supplied facts from illustrative copy. Prices, specs, guarantees, reviews, medical or financial claims must come from the user or be clearly marked as placeholders to verify before publishing. Realistic-looking data does not make it true.

## Localization

Design for text expansion (German and Indonesian often run 20–35% longer than English), right-to-left layouts, plurals, local date/currency formats, and user-entered names. Keep headlines short enough to survive translation.
